# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data {{publisher}} collects when you use the Life
Pixel hosted service, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. It does not cover the
desktop app or a self-hosted server, which send us nothing unless whoever runs them chooses to
turn telemetry on.

## Data we collect

- **Account data**: your email address, a password hash — never your password itself —, your
  chosen language and plan.
- **Your creations**: the animations and images you save.
- **Product events**: pseudonymous usage events (for instance, which feature was used), without
  their content — never the pixels of an animation.

We collect no more than this: no third-party analytics, no advertising identifier, no device
fingerprinting.

## Why we collect it, and on what basis

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | create, secure and support your account | performance of the contract these Terms form |
| Your creations | save, display, export and, if you choose, publish them as a live embed | performance of the contract |
| Product events | understand which features are used, and improve the product | our legitimate interest, balanced by keeping events pseudonymous and short-lived |

## How long we keep it

- Product events: 13 months.
- A deleted account: purged within 30 days, then gone from backups as they rotate, within six
  months.
- Access logs at the network's edge — the IP address and the geolocation derived from it, which
  are personal data —: 90 days.

## Who processes it, and where

| Data | Processor | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Your creations, stored | Scaleway Object Storage | Paris, France (EU) |
| Database backups | Scaleway Object Storage | Amsterdam, Netherlands (EU) |
| Transactional email (account and security notices) | Scaleway Transactional Email | EU |

We prefer processors located in the EU. A self-hosted server sends this data nowhere but its own
infrastructure, chosen by whoever runs it — {{host}} for the server answering this page.

## Your rights

From the app, or through a support request, you can:

- **access** your data: export everything attached to your account;
- **erase** your data: delete your account, including what is stored in object storage.

## The one cookie

The hosted service sets exactly one cookie: the session cookie, strictly necessary to keep you
signed in. It serves no advertising or tracking purpose, so it needs no consent banner. We use no
other cookie and no third-party tracker.
